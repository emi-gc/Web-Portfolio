  • Province/State: Capital Region
  • Country: Iceland
  • Population: Approximately 139,800
  • Latitude & longitude: 64.1466° N, 21.9426° W

Takayama

Takayama is a mountain city in Gifu Prefecture. It is known for its preserved old town, traditional wooden buildings, morning markets, local food, and seasonal festivals. I have been here before, it seems like a small and quiet town but it is a lovely place, stayed at a traditional hotel.

  • Province/State: Gifu Prefecture
  • Country: Japan
  • Population: Approximately 81,968
  • Latitude & longitude: 36.1461° N, 137.2522° E
